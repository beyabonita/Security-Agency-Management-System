# 1.4 Scope and Limitations

## Scope of the Study
This study focused on the design, development, and implementation of the **Web-Based Security Agency Management System with Mobile Application for Real-Time Monitoring** for **Twenty Twenty Security Agency**, modernizing its administrative workflows, guard deployment, attendance verification, and field incident reporting. The system consists of an integrated cloud-hosted web portal for agency leadership and a specialized mobile application for frontline security personnel.

The web management platform incorporates three distinct role-based supervisory consoles:
1. **IT Administrator Console (`it_admin`):** Governs platform-wide infrastructure, sets global configuration defaults (such as default geofence radius in meters, agency broadcast announcements, and support contact routing), monitors system diagnostic health (database latency, cloud storage connectivity, and authentication status), and manages administrative account lifecycles (creation, status activation, temporary password issuance, and role provisioning).
2. **Operations Head Console (`admin`):** Serves as the central operational hub for agency management. It allows the Operations Head to maintain the Personnel Masterlist (for security guards and field inspectors) with automated duplicate mobile number detection and input validation; configure client deployment sites with unique geographic coordinates and interactive geofence boundaries (radius in meters); generate 24-hour duty shift schedules (Day, Night, and customized duty windows) with automated scheduling conflict detection and double-shift prevention; adjust and edit shift times in real time; evaluate, approve, or reject categorized guard duty requests (*Absence Requests, Shift Swap Requests, and Overtime Requests*); investigate real-time incident reports, assign severity levels, assess attached multimedia evidence, log administrative findings, and update case dispositions (*Under Investigation, Escalated, Resolved*); monitor deployed personnel in real time using the **Guard Tracker**, featuring transmission age indicators, structured client roster categorization (*Currently Online* vs. *Other Assigned Guards · Different Shift Times*), and historical patrol breadcrumbs (**Location History**); and automatically compile verified attendance records to generate standardized Daily Time Records (DTRs) exportable into auditable PDF timesheets and CSV summaries for payroll processing.
3. **Field Inspector Console (`inspector`):** Provides field supervisors with agency-wide operational oversight across all active guards, deployment sites, shift schedules, and emergency alerts without restrictive 1-to-1 guard assignment barriers. Inspectors utilize the console to assess field incident submissions, verify physical evidence, record supervisory disposition notes, review active duty rosters, audit guard DTR records, and track guard locations via the Guard Tracker to coordinate unannounced spot inspections and verify post compliance.

The **Guard Mobile Application** (developed using the Flutter framework for Android devices) provides frontline personnel with the following capabilities:
1. **Duty Awareness:** Instant access to assigned weekly duty schedules, post assignments, and shift times.
2. **Geofence-Restricted Attendance Timekeeping:** Real-time Time-In and Time-Out clocking strictly restricted to the authorized geographic perimeter of the guard’s assigned client post, automatically cross-referencing live GPS coordinates against pre-configured geofence radii.
3. **Biometric and Hardware Anti-Tampering Security:**
   - **Live In-App Camera Face Capture:** Requires live facial photo capture directly through the mobile camera during Time-In and Time-Out punches, strictly prohibiting gallery selection, pre-recorded photos, or proxy clock-ins (*buddy punching*).
   - **Hardware and Cellular Device Binding:** Locks the guard’s mobile user account to their registered smartphone hardware identifier (`device_id`, `device_locked`, and cellular network attributes) to ensure authentication occurs only on authorized physical devices.
   - **Geotagged Watermark Stamping:** Permanently encodes real-time GPS latitude and longitude coordinates, date, timestamp, guard name, and client post name directly onto evidence photos.
   - **Dual-Point Proximity Verification:** Performs distance checks between guard locations and designated assembly points for official seminars, briefings, or post deployments.
   - **Force Attendance Exception Workflow:** Accommodates emergency or off-site deployment duties outside standard boundaries by flagging off-site punches as pending requests, requiring explicit review and approval by the Operations Head.
4. **Digital Incident Reporting:** Allows guards to file immediate emergency reports categorized by incident type (*Crime/Theft, Fire/Hazard, Medical Emergency, Trespassing, Alarm Trigger*), accompanied by descriptive narratives, exact GPS coordinates, and captured camera photos or video recordings for immediate administrative escalation.
5. **Duty Request Filing:** Enables guards to formally submit Absence Requests, Shift Swap Requests (specifying substitute guards with mutual consent), and Overtime Requests with uploaded formal excuse letters and supporting documentation.
6. **Attendance & DTR Self-Monitoring:** Provides guards with transparent views of cumulative rendered duty hours, punctuality logs, and monthly attendance records.

## Limitations of the Study
1. **Workforce and Operational Delimitation:** The scope of this study is strictly confined to operational workforce administration, deployment scheduling, geofenced attendance tracking, duty request workflows, and digital incident documentation. It does **not** encompass automated payroll disbursement, statutory benefits deductions (e.g., SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG), income tax withholding (BIR), net pay calculations, or direct integration with third-party commercial banking APIs. The system outputs verified duty hours, overtime tallies, and attendance logs ready for administrative import into external accounting or payroll software.
2. **Platform and Operating System Constraints:** The mobile application is engineered exclusively for mobile devices running the **Android operating system (version 8.0 Oreo and higher)**; native iOS (Apple) client software is not covered. The web management consoles are designed for standard modern web browsers (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox) accessed via desktop or laptop computers.
3. **Hardware, Sensor, and Connectivity Dependencies:** The accuracy and reliability of geofence perimeter validation and real-time GPS tracking depend directly on the physical specifications of the guard’s mobile device, including the built-in GPS receiver, hardware location sensors, location permissions, and satellite reception. Environmental factors such as dense indoor posts, underground structures, or severe weather conditions may affect positioning precision. Furthermore, continuous real-time tracking, live notifications, and database synchronization require active, stable mobile data (4G/5G) or Wi-Fi internet connectivity.
4. **Armory and Weaponry Boundaries:** The system’s firearm and ammunition module is strictly limited to administrative record-keeping, serial number logging, inspection records, and duty post allocation monitoring. It does not include automated smart armory hardware, electronic locking gun racks, biometric dispensing lockers, physical ballistic testing, weapon maintenance, or repair operations.
5. **Organizational and Contextual Scope:** The system’s operational policies, shift paradigms (such as 12-hour rotating shifts and 24-hour site coverage), post quotas, and supervisory hierarchies are tailored specifically to the operating requirements of **Twenty Twenty Security Agency** in Negros Occidental. Adoption by other private security agencies may require configuration adjustments to align with differing organizational protocols and contractual client commitments.
