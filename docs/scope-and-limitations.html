1.4 Scope and Limitations

This study focused on building an integrated security management system. The system included a web portal for administrators and supervisors, and a mobile application for security guards. The web portal allowed the IT Administrator to manage administrator accounts, set default system settings, and check overall platform health. It allowed the Operations Head to register guard and inspector profiles, set up client deployment sites with interactive geofence boundaries, create 24-hour guard duty schedules with flexible shift time adjustments, approve or reject duty requests such as absence and shift swaps, review incident reports with photos and videos, monitor on-duty guards in real time using the Guard Tracker with location history, and generate Daily Time Records (DTR) that saved directly to the schedule database. The mobile application allowed security guards to view their work schedules, time in and out for attendance within site boundaries, perform live in-app camera face capture to verify presence, bind their account to their registered mobile device to prevent proxy clock-ins, file categorized duty requests with attached letters, and send emergency incident reports with photos, videos, and GPS location. Inspectors used the system with agency-wide access to view work schedules, check site profiles, review incident reports, view or download DTR files, and monitor on-duty guards on the Guard Tracker during field inspections.

This study is limited to operational workforce management, deployment scheduling, geofenced attendance tracking, duty requests, and field incident documentation. The mobile application is designed for Android devices only. The system does not include full financial accounting, automated payroll processing, tax computations, or third-party banking integrations. Attendance verification depends on the GPS receiver, location settings, and camera capabilities of the guard’s mobile device, while real-time synchronization and live tracking depend on stable mobile data or Wi-Fi connectivity. The system’s firearm and ammunition feature is limited to inspection records, serial number logging, and post monitoring, and does not cover the physical handling, testing, repair, maintenance, or issuance of firearms and ammunition. The system was designed according to the operational procedures, shift structures, and post assignment requirements of Twenty Twenty Security Agency; therefore, other security agencies may require configuration changes or additional adjustments to meet their specific operational policies and requirements.
